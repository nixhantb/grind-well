## What GrindWell actually is

GrindWell is a **local-first spaced-repetition trainer for turning recognized patterns into typed-from-memory solutions** its whole job is drilling the *implementation* into your hands until it's automatic, using the same interval-based repetition that works for vocabulary, music, and every other motor/memory skill.
* **24 patterns, ~149 problems:** Follow a fixed order: template first, then problems.
* **Retyping protocol:** Retype every accepted solution from a blank file at +0, +1, +3, +7, and +21 days.
* **90-second stall rule:** Peek for 20 seconds, then restart the entire method from its signature.
* **Blank-page ritual:** Start with signature, dummy return, I/O notes, and a brute-force skeleton.
* **Pseudocode + 20-minute ladder:** Use structured checkpoints from brute force to optimization to editorial.
* **Graduation logic:** Graduate after 3 consecutive clean reps; failed/assisted reps reset to +1 day.
* **Cold reproduction rate:** Track clean reps ÷ total attempts as the primary retention metric.
* **Stuck-line tracking:** Log every stalled line to uncover your recurring personal weak spots.
* **Local-first storage:** Keep everything in browser storage with JSON export/import for backups and syncing.
